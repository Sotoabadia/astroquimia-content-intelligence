Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 28, 2026

Astroquimia Content Intelligence is a private content analytics tool designed to organize and analyze content and performance data from authorized social media accounts.

Data accessed

When an account owner explicitly authorizes access through TikTok, the application may access information permitted by the scopes granted, including basic account information, public video information and associated video performance metrics made available by TikTok.

How data is used

The data is used to organize content, analyze video performance, identify editorial patterns and support content decisions for the authorized account.

Data sharing

TikTok user data obtained through the integration is not sold. The application is intended for use with accounts explicitly authorized by their owner.

Data storage

Application data may be stored locally for content analysis and historical performance comparison.

Revoking access

Users may revoke the application's access to their TikTok account through the authorization and account settings provided by TikTok.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests concerning Astroquimia Content Intelligence, please contact the application owner through the Astroquimia official channels.
